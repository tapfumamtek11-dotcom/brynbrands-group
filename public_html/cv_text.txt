Brief Music Bio…  
 
          BRYN TAURAI MTEKI a.k.a. Sekurutau  
 
Bryn was born in Harare Zimbabwe in 1975 on 18 October, and is the son of 
renowned sculptor Richard Mteki.Bryn himself is a well traveled world famous 
sculptor .Creativity has always been his family’s inheritance, beginning with his late 
uncle Boira Mteki who is one of the first generation Zimbabwean Sculptors to be 
recognized worldwide .His creativity produced compilation of music of the African 
origin since his artworks also portray some reality of the African society. So to speak, 
the music is like a mov ement from and to the artworks which have been already 
been meaningful to everyone who comes across it. For music has been always a 
dream for Bryn, . His music, is product of his perseverance to bring variety to true 
Zimbabwean traditional arts from the st one medium to fine Melody ,His long time 
friend American musician  R.Kelly  encouraged him to try and put voices to his 
impressive sculptures through complementing his talent with singing.  
As a man who always takes advice, Sekurutau gave a try in the new art and the 
venture unexpectedly turned to gold.  
His first album Kurauone which was released in 2000 went platinum and it heralded 
the arrival of a new talented kid on the mbira music block.  
African Roots music is his style that is heavy with emphatic mbira melodies which are 
well fused with his deep voice that has become a common and popular feature of the 
Zimbabwean music scene.  
 – Blessing Banda, Music Critic

Bryn Taurai Mteki a.k.a.SEKURUTAU AUTOBIOGRAPH  
 by Godwin Muzari (Herald reporter) November 13,  2004  
 
He has seen it all in the music and sculpture industries and has conquered the world 
through his superb dual combination of amazing vocal talent and exceptional stone -
carving expertise.  
At 29, Sekurutau has achieved what has remained a pipe -dream for most 
Zimbabwean artists of his age.  
Born Bryn Taurai Mteki, Sekurutau grew up in Harare’s high -density suburb of 
Highfield and was accustomed to seeing his father Richard, wield a chisel everyday 
as he carved excellent sculptures that still stand in various galleries worldwide.  
 Sekurutau admired his father’s pieces and was determined to try his hand in the 
sculpting art since his boyhood such that by the time he turned 11, he was already 
producing attractive pieces.  
It became very clear that Sekurutau was destined for greater heights since his 
imposing sculptures approximated a seasoned sculptor’s work.  
Many who saw his pieces could not believe that the hand of such a young child was 
doing such great wonders that left even world class stone -carvers perplexed.  
There was no doubt that young Bryn had not only learnt from observing his father 
work but the art came naturally. The legacy that his gifted late uncle Boira Mteki - 
who was also a renowned sculptor – had left now ran in the family blood.  
When Sekurutau completed his Ordinary level, it did not take him second thoughts to 
venture into full -time stone -carving.  
He made history by becoming Zimbabwe’s youngest professional sculptor and his 
achievements were honored in a number of awards that he has received worldwide.  
In 1996 he was awarded a silver medal by the then Mayoress of Oelsnitz              
Eva Maria Mabious  in Germany while the following year he scooped another two 
medals in Auerbach an Adorf in the same country where the Mayors of both cities 
handed him the  honors.  
The Oelsnitz medal was a great success story for the then young sculptor as he was 
the first African to receive such an honor for his status to bring sculpting to the town. 
Such a medal, which  young  Mteki was honored  with ,is  a limited production  of 
100 pieces given as honors to the “World’s  Rich and Famous” who visits that  town .  
 
Sekurutau has numerous certificates from around the world notably from Germany 
and the United States.  
Most of the certificates were awarded him in recognition of his outstanding 
performances in exhibitions and workshops that he has extensively held in various 
countries.  
  The exhibitions date back to 1992  when Bryn  became a fulltime  artist  when he 
was still at school when he took part in the Zimbabwe Heritage Exhibition which was 
held at the National gallery of Zimbabwe. His works were displayed in Zimbabwe  
Heritage  Exhibition in 1992. He was the youngest  artist to have his sculptures  
selected out of work received country wide.  
In 1993 his pieces were displayed at the Harare International Conference Centre in 
the African Environment Expo.  
Thereafter, most of his exhibitions have been held outside Zimbabwe and this has 
seen his works finding their way into private collections and galleries in countries like 
Spain, Belgium, France, Norway, Japan, Australia, Sweden ,Germany, South Africa  a 
Netherlands and world over .

Unlike his father who enjoyed carving images of living objects, Sekurutau explores 
much more contemporary themes of love, relationships, conversations and other 
social issues.  
His expertise saw him being commissioned to sculpt a symbol for the 1995 All Africa 
Games that were held in Zimbabwe and this saw him coming up with “Chippi”, a 
piece that still stands at the National Sports Stadium as a permanent collection.  
Sekurutau has indeed taken the work started by his father and uncle to dizzy  
Heights His worldwide tours and exhibitions saw him rubbing shoulders , mixing and 
mingling with the world’s most famous figures who include R Kelly, Mike Tyson, 
Wesley Snipes, Michael Jordan, Andy Cole, Austin “Jay -Jay” Okocha, Burning 
Spear(Winston Rodne y), Kirsty Coventry, Oliver Mtukudzi, Dr. Thomas Mapfumo and 
Rev. Jessie Jackson among others.  
It was from such interactions that Sekurutau’s interest in music arose.  
His long time friend R Kelly encouraged him to try and put voices to his impressive 
sculptures through complementing his talent with singing.  
As a man who always takes advice, Sekurutau gave a try in the new art and the 
venture unexpectedly turned to gold.  
His first album Kurauone which was released in 2000 went platinum and it heralded 
the arrival of a new talented kid on the mbira music block.  
African Roots music is his style that is heavy with emphatic mbira melodies which are 
well fused with his deep voice that has become a common and popular feature of the 
Zimbabwean music scene.He also released Kuyaruka and Ndakura before coming up 
with an i mpressive Mwana Wevhu.Sekurutau rose to success with every release and 
his spectecular live show performances saw him making a mark in the country and 
leaving lasting impressions abroad.  
In 2003 he released  Pfugama Unamate  and  Hamunyare then 2004 Tsuro Magen’a 
this time everyone got the conviction that Sekurutau was indeed the mbira musician 
of the moment.he album rocked the music scene and left many marveling at the 
incredible fusion o f artistic talents that has made Sekurutau a trademark name.  
 His latest album – Vadzoka – is destined for even greater heights as it is pregnant 
with maturity and amazing invaluable lyrical content.  
The album features a rising musical sensation in the name of Selmar Mtukudzi, 
daughter of another Zimbabwean superstar Oliver Mtukudzi.  
Sekurutau has all that it takes to make even more difference to the world of music.  
 
Now Bryn Taurai Mteki a.k.a.  Sekurutau (Sculptor/Musician) is  an accomplished 
Businessman. Director and  Founder of   Mteki World Art (Private) Limited Group of 
Companies , which BrynBrands is a subsidiary t/a BrynBrands Supermarket , 
BrynBrands Butchuri es, Bottle Stores , Mini Markets , Take aways, Night Clubs 
including Club Saratoga , Sekurutau Gallery, Sekurutau World Music, Taurai 
Transport,  Aust -Mteki Foundation, Aust -Mteki Technologies, BryBen Motors, Vision II 
& MANA  Gold Mines and a Clothing  li ne and retail Haus Of Sekurutau .  
 
Recently Bryn Taurai Mteki aka Sekurutau made History as first ever  Artist 
to  participate as the only INDEPENDENT Presidential Candidate for 2018 
Zimbabwe Harmonized Elections Garnering 0.1%  among other 23 
presidential candidates. Also back in 2002 he c ontested as a Member of  
Parliament for Highfields Constituency  before paving way for War veteran 
Cde Joseph Chinotimba due to commitments back in the United States of 
America.

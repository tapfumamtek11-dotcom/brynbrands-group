The Artist

Bryn Taurai Mteki

The stone already contains the spirit. My hands simply reveal what has always been there.

Known by his artistic name Sekurutau-Dr Hon Bryn Taurai Mteki stands as one of Zimbabwe's most distinguished sculptors. A second-generation master, he inherited not merely technique but a profound philosophical approach to stone.

Born in 1975 in Harare, Zimbabwe, Mteki began carving at the age of 11 under the guidance of his father, renowned sculptor Richard Mteki.

Since his first international exhibition in 1992, Mteki's work has graced galleries and private collections across four continents. His sculptures—carved primarily from serpentine, springstone, and opal stone—explore themes of leadership, love, spiritual transcendence, and the contemporary African experience.

Collecting & Commissions

Each Sekurutau sculpture represents months of meditative work—a dialogue between artist, stone, and spirit. Works are acquired through private consultation, ensuring collectors receive pieces that resonate with their personal journey.

For collectors seeking a unique commission, Mteki accepts a limited number of bespoke projects annually. These collaborative works begin with deep conversation about meaning, space, and intention.

The Acquisition Process

  • i Initial consultation to understand your vision and requirements
  • ii Private viewing at the gallery or digital presentation of available works
  • iii Provenance documentation and certificate of authenticity
  • iv White-glove international shipping with full insurance
  • v Installation guidance and ongoing collector relationship

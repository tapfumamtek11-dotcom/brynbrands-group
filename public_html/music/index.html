The Dual Genius

From Stone Medium to Fine Melody

"The music is like a movement from and to the artworks which have already been meaningful to everyone who comes across it."

Born into a family of legendary sculptors—as the son of master Richard Mteki and nephew of world-celebrated first-generation sculptor Boira Mteki—Bryn Taurai Mteki has sculpted since the age of 11. In 1995 he was commissioned to sculpt "Chippi," the famous 2.5m permanent mascot for the 6th All Africa Games standing at the National Sports Stadium.

During his extensive international tours, music was always a deep internal calling. Encouraged by longtime friend and American musician R. Kelly to complement his sculptures with singing, Sekurutau gave voice to his impressive stones. His 2000 debut album Kurauone went platinum, establishing him immediately as a giant of contemporary mbira and African roots music.

With Sahwira Mukuru, Sekurutau returns with full emotional and artistic maturity, celebrating enduring friendship, honouring his late mother, and uplifting African spiritual identity.

Artist Liner Notes

Sahwira Mukuru • In the Artist's Own Words

"A great comeback as I celebrate friendship, honour my late mother, and embrace my African Roots through music."

Sahwira Mukuru track honours masahwira makuru, as we need them at the end of time as we depart from this world.

Amai Hwee pays tribute to my late mother, Tabeth Elizabeth Chikwenya, whose memory lives on in my heart. May her soul rest in peace.

Rugare Rwauya encourages everyone to live pretty and enjoy life.

Musikavanhu gives thanks to the Almighty for the gift of life, while Svaa Makudo reminds the younger generation of the ancient world and our rich cultural heritage.

Finally, Rovambira confirms the deep roots of African music in me as we usher in our new Rovambira Dance. This album is a celebration of friendship, love, faith, life after life (in which masahwira plays a great part as we depart this world, hence the name of this album), and African identity.

Ndatenda hangu. Yours in music,

Sekurutau

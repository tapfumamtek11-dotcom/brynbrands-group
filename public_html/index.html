BrynBrands Group (Private) Limited stands as a registered multi-sector solutions, trading, and investment holding company. Since our establishment in 2014, we have consistently delivered value across diverse sectors, partnering with government institutions, corporate entities, and international collaborators.

Our strategic positioning enables us to identify opportunities, mobilize resources, and execute projects that drive sustainable economic growth. We operate with the understanding that African enterprise, when properly structured and professionally managed, can compete and excel on the global stage.

Our Philosophy

"Pachedu" — meaning "together" in Shona — embodies our belief that sustainable solutions emerge from collaborative partnerships. We bridge the gap between ambition and execution, connecting stakeholders across sectors to create lasting value.

Dr. Hon. Bryn Taurai Mteki

Founder • Acclaimed Visionary • Sculptor • Musician • Strategist

Born into a legendary dynasty of soapstone sculptors, Dr. Mteki (popularly known as Sekuru Tau) has spent over 25 years bridging the world of fine art with Pan-African enterprise. From the boardrooms of the National Gallery of Zimbabwe to international music stages, he embodies the "Pachedu" spirit of collaborative excellence.

Legacy of Iconic Artistry

Creator of "Chippi," the beloved 1995 All-Africa Games mascot, and a key figure on the Board of Trustees for the National Gallery of Zimbabwe.

Community Impact Focus

Committed to creating sustainable economic opportunities that benefit local communities and stakeholders.

Cultural Ambassador

A powerhouse in the "African Roots" music genre, known for chart-topping hits like "Nora" that resonate across the SADC region.
